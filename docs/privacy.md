# Privacy Policy — EvoClash

Effective date: 2026-08-14

This Privacy Policy explains how EvoClash (the “App”), collects, uses, and shares information when you use the App or related services. The App is owned and operated by the app owner (contact: evo.clash1916@gmail.com). If you have questions about privacy or want to request data access or deletion, contact that email.

## 1. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

- Account information you provide: email address, username, password (stored securely), and profile preferences.
- Payment and purchase records: we use Stripe for web payments. We do not store raw credit card numbers; Stripe handles card data. We store purchase receipts, product IDs, transaction IDs, and entitlement records in our server-side ledger (iap_purchases and CoinTransaction) for entitlement fulfillment and reconciliation.
- Purchase receipts from Apple: when you make in‑app purchases via the App Store, the App uploads receipts to our server for validation and entitlement recording.
- Usage data and diagnostics: anonymous or pseudonymous analytics, crash logs, device model, operating system version, and in‑game telemetry used to troubleshoot and improve the game.
- Optional content: user-uploaded images or profile content only if you explicitly submit them.

## 2. How we collect information

- Directly from you when you sign up, log in, or make purchases.
- Automatically from your device when you use the App (analytics, crash reports, device metadata).
- From third-party providers (Stripe for payment processing, and Apple for receipt verification).

## 3. Purposes for which we use data

We use collected information to:

- Provide and maintain the App and services (account management, entitlements, purchases).
- Process and validate purchases and grant entitlements (server-side receipt validation for App Store receipts; Stripe for web purchases).
- Improve the App based on analytics and crash reporting.
- Prevent fraud and abuse.
- Respond to user support requests and legal obligations.

## 4. Third‑party processors

We use the following third‑party services which may process your data:

- Stripe — payment processing. Stripe’s Privacy Policy: https://stripe.com/privacy
- Apple — App Store receipt validation and in‑app purchases. Apple’s privacy pages: https://developer.apple.com/support/privacy/
- Analytics / crash reporting (if enabled) — links to their privacy policies will be disclosed in the app settings if used.

We do not share card numbers with our servers; Stripe handles sensitive payment data.

## 5. Sharing & disclosure

We will not sell your personal information. We may disclose data to:

- Service providers and processors (such as Stripe and analytics providers) to perform services on our behalf.
- Law enforcement or regulators where required by law.
- Successors in the event of a merger or acquisition (with notice where required).

## 6. Retention

We retain account and purchase records for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, and support your requests (typically X years — configurable by the operator). If you request deletion, we will remove your personal data from active systems and retain only aggregated or anonymized records as required for legal compliance.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data or to restrict or object to processing. To exercise these rights, contact evo.clash1916@gmail.com. We will respond according to applicable law.

## 8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

## 9. Security

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. Sensitive credentials and keys are stored in secure secrets stores and are never committed to the code repository.

## 10. International transfers

Data may be processed in countries outside your residence. We will take steps to ensure transfers comply with applicable law.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy; we will post the updated policy at the same location and update the effective date. Significant changes will be communicated via the App or other available means.
